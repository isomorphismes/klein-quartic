# The Klein quartic from `*237`: finite quotient and hyperbolic covering

The [Conway `hyperbolic237` experiment](https://github.com/isomorphismes/Conway/tree/main/hyperbolic237)
constructs the `(2,3,7)` Schwarz triangle in the Poincaré disk, reflects it,
and associates every drawn chamber with an **exact** element of PGL(2,F7).
The exact matrices are not obtained by rounding geometric coordinates.

## The 24-heptagon regular map

The full triangle-reflection group surjects onto PGL(2,7) (336 elements).
Its orientation-preserving subgroup surjects onto PSL(2,7) (168 elements).
The torsion-free kernel of the latter quotient gives the Klein quartic's
compact genus-three hyperbolic surface.

A regular heptagon in the universal `{7,3}` tessellation is the union of
14 `(2,3,7)` Schwarz triangles around a `π/7` corner. The corresponding
stabilizer in the finite quotient is the dihedral group generated by the two
reflections meeting at that corner. There are **336 / 14 = 24** heptagon cosets.

An orientably regular map can be constructed on the 168 elements of PSL(2,7)
regarded as directed edges (darts). With U=[1 1;0 1], V=[0 -1;1 0], take
right multiplication by U for the **face** permutation, V for **edge**
reversal, and (VU)^−1 for **vertex** rotation. These satisfy the map's
incidence identity and have cycle lengths 7,2,3 respectively.

| Map structure | Number |
| --- | ---: |
| Heptagonal faces | 24 |
| Edges | 84 |
| Vertices | 56 |
| Directed edges | 168 |
| Schwarz triangles | 336 |
| Euler characteristic | −4 |
| Genus | 3 |

The quotient area at curvature −1 is 336 × (π/42) = 8π. This agrees with
Gauss–Bonnet, 4π(g−1) at g=3.

## What can actually be reused

This provides **verified combinatorial gluing permutations** and a numeric
universal-cover patch, but not yet a specific hyperbolic 14-gon with an
explicit boundary-pairing table. It does not equate points on Tim Hutton's
existing 3D embedding, nor turn the old Python 2/VTK model into a modern
renderer. The mapping of the 24 quotient faces to Hutton's 24 embedded
heptagons is a separate correspondence problem. In particular, do not treat
its partially filled `plane_to_kq` map as complete.

Next geometric experiment: compute a connected 336-triangle fundamental
region for the torsion-free kernel, extract paired oriented boundary edges,
and verify those pairings against the exact 168-dart permutations before
considering 3D deformation. This is a distinct requirement from coloring a
patch by coset labels.
